Riemann Sketchpad

A desktop application for creating geometric constructions on the Riemann sphere using compass and straightedge in elliptic geometry.

Download

Also available: Euclid Sketchpad and Poincare Sketchpad — the Euclidean and hyperbolic geometry versions.

Features

  • Compass-and-straightedge tools — construct points, lines, segments, circles, angles, and regions on the sphere
  • Dynamic geometry — drag free points and watch the entire construction update in real time
  • Intersection detection — automatic intersection previews between great circles and circles
  • Measurements — measure spherical distances, angles, circle circumferences, and region areas
  • Custom constructions — record a sequence of steps, save it, and reapply it with different inputs
  • Built-in constructions — midpoint, perpendicular line, and reflection point
  • Orthographic projection — view the sphere from any angle with azimuth/elevation camera controls
  • Export — save diagrams as PNG or SVG with canvas or custom crop methods
  • Undo/redo — full snapshot-based undo history
